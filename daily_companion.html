When you feel watched
Take a beat. Look in any direction other than the one you feel "watched" from. Notice that other people are looking at their own reflections, their phones, the ceiling. You were peripherally registered, not focally observed.
When you don't know how to use a machine
Every machine has a sticker with a diagram. Read it. Take 30 seconds. If you're still unsure, do an empty rep — just move the pads through the motion with no weight to see what it does.
Looking unfamiliar with a machine for 60 seconds is invisible to everyone but you.
When you can't finish a set
Drop the weight. There's no shame in a drop set. Finishing the prescribed reps with a lighter weight is more valuable than failing reps you didn't have.
When you don't want to go
Make the rule: "Just put the gym clothes on." That's the only commitment.
90% of the time, once they're on, you go. The remaining 10% is a real signal you need a rest day. Take it without guilt.
